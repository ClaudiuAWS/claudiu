\textit{Watching a live game with a friend is fun. Reacting to it together in real time is even better.}

\bigskip
\noindent Bundesliga Brezn is a real-time multiplayer web app that turns 
passive match-watching into a shared, competitive experience between two fans. 
Fans have always watched matches together, but digital fan products treat each 
user in isolation. Bundesliga Brezn turns watching football into something you 
actually play.

Before kick-off, fans draft their starting lineup from the players available in 
that day's match — competing against a friend to build the best squad. The app 
encourages users to build mixed squads from both clubs, meaning no two lineups 
will ever look the same. As the game unfolds, your chosen players earn you points 
in real time: your striker scores, you climb the leaderboard — but if your 
goalkeeper concedes or one of your players is booked, you lose points too. 
Between the action, minigames keep the energy up — test your offside reflexes, 
answer trivia, or go head-to-head in a penalty shootout where one friend shoots 
and another defends.

The goal is simple: make every matchday feel like it was worth showing up for.

\section*{Frequently Asked Questions}

\subsection*{Customer FAQs}

\textbf{Do I need an account to join a Bundesliga Brezn session?}

Yes, you do need an account. Thankfully it is easy to create — just enter your 
email, pick a password, and confirm your address with the code you receive. 
You're in within minutes.

\textbf{How many friends can I play with at once?}

Currently Bundesliga Brezn supports 1v1 matches. Support for larger groups 
is planned as a future extension.

\textbf{How does team drafting work?}

Once you and your friend have created a party and both mark yourselves as ready, 
the draft begins. Each round presents both players with a choice between two 
players of identical or similar positions. If you and your opponent pick 
different players, each of you keeps their own. If you both pick the same player, 
a coinflip decides who gets them — the loser receives the player neither side chose.

After the draft, you select which players make your starting eleven — the app 
automatically identifies a valid real football formation and positions your 
players on the pitch accordingly, no manual arrangement needed. Once you lock 
in your squad, you must select a captain: your captain earns you double points 
for every event they are involved in. The app encourages building mixed squads 
from both clubs, ensuring every lineup is unique.

\textbf{How do I earn points?}

Points are earned on specific match events — your striker scores, your 
goalkeeper makes a save, one of your players wins a tackle. However, points 
can also be lost: if your goalkeeper concedes or one of your players receives 
a yellow card, your score takes a hit. Reaction events also appear on the right 
side of the screen during spectacular plays and nutmegs — react quickly enough 
and you earn bonus points.

\textbf{What do I do with points?}

Points can be used to purchase customisable music discs that play specific songs 
while you are in the app, or perks that give you an advantage over your opponent 
in future matches.

\textbf{How do minigames work?}

Minigames appear throughout the match to keep things competitive between events. 
There are three types:

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Offside Reflex} --- A player walks across the pitch and you must 
    react the moment they cross the offside line. The faster your reaction, the 
    more points you earn.

    \item \textbf{Penalty Shootout} --- Two players from your party go head-to-head: 
    one shoots, one defends. The striker picks a side to shoot, the goalkeeper picks 
    a side to dive — if they differ, the striker scores; if they match, the keeper saves.

    \item \textbf{Trivia} --- Football knowledge questions appear at regular intervals 
    throughout the match. Answer correctly and quickly to earn bonus points.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{Technical \& Internal FAQs}

\textbf{How does the app stay in sync during a live match?}

WebSocket connections are used throughout the app whenever live updates need 
to happen. Persistent storage in the AWS cloud ensures that your data is not 
lost if you accidentally refresh the page or exit and return.

\textbf{What happens if a user loses connection mid-game?}

They can simply reconnect. All game state is persistently stored in DynamoDB, 
so nothing is lost if a player drops out and rejoins mid-match.

\textbf{How are live events processed without race conditions or timing issues?}

A fully cloud-native approach on AWS ensures events are handled in strict order. 
EventBridge feeds into an SQS FIFO queue, guaranteeing that match events are 
delivered and processed in exactly the sequence they occur — keeping every 
connected client's experience consistent and true to the live match.

\textbf{Can the application handle sudden traffic spikes?}

Yes. The entire solution is serverless, leveraging AWS Lambda, DynamoDB 
on-demand, S3, and other managed services that scale automatically with demand.

\textbf{How is artificial intelligence integrated into the app?}

AWS Bedrock is used to generate live match commentary throughout the game. 
As events unfold --- a goal, a red card, a last-minute substitution --- 
Bedrock interprets the event data and produces a short, natural-language 
comment that appears in the app, giving the experience a broadcast-style 
voice without requiring a human commentator. This keeps the atmosphere 
alive between minigames and reinforces the feeling that something is always 
happening.
